# Open Spend by Riamu Technologies

An invoicing payment software to manage and track payments for your next business or app.

## What is OpenSpend?

OpenSpend is an open-source payment gateway for your next app, project, or business. It lets end users send money using their online banking directly to businesses using their online banking app or website. And businesses can check live and instantly whether the money has been deposited or not.

No credit card required.

No login to bank required.
